## 3. What to submit, and how to think about it

Under best-of with unlimited submissions, a submission can only raise the standing, so the question
is expected value per upload and honesty about noise. Every candidate here correlates 0.96 to 0.99
with v2ens per image; the paired SD of two such candidates on 100 images is 0.05 to 0.07, so the
spread between them on the hidden set is mostly draw noise, not method. Submitting several and
keeping the best is selecting on the test set: allowed by the rules, worth perhaps +0.05 to +0.07 on
the max of four or five tries, and it will not transfer to Phase II's 1,000 images (paired SD 0.02).

Suggested order, one at a time, recording each score in `code/experiments/ledger_manual.yaml`
(`rows:` with `split: platform100`) before the next:

1. `plus15`: the only configuration with a confirmed OOF gain over v2ens (-0.025, CI excludes zero)
   that does not carry the unvalidatable refits. It also tells you, together with the 8.8531 result,
   whether the refits or the Base folds hurt on this draw.
2. `refit12`: v2ens plus the two V2-Tiny refits. Expected +0.02 on the OOF scale; the 8.8531 result
   makes its sign on this draw uncertain.
3. `v2tiny5` and `v2base5`: single-family measurements; lower expected value, most decorrelated.
4. `v2only7` only if 1 to 3 all land below v2ens.

Upload path (the one that is known to work): open the algorithm "clear ec phase1 v2ens plus
selfcontained" (image `38863e52`), upload the Model archive, make it the active Model version, run

## 7. Phase II (opens 1 October; 1,000 images; paired visibility 0.03 to 0.05)

The only lever with measured headroom is CD from detected cell geometry: CD given the clicks has
0.8 percent scatter, the annotator-window emulation costs under 1 percent, the S1 detector reads
5.3 percent inside the true click hull on overlay-like frames but 10.1 percent on the population
because 19 click sets from one series do not transfer. Ranked levers (see the S1/S2/S3 reports for
the evidence):

| lever | fixes | cost | expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| more click sets across series: correct the detector's peaks on 60-100 frames from every ID family and CD tertile in the viewer | the transfer failure (the whole 4-point gap) | 3-5 human hours + 1 GPU hour | the only route from CD 10 to 5 on the population |
| click-noise-tolerant target (matching loss or offset head) and NMS by predicted local spacing | 3-7 px localisation, clicks closer than the fixed NMS | 2-4 GPU hours | 1-2 CD points on frames it already handles |
| count-consistent pseudo-labels (threshold per frame so the window count matches the label-implied count; keep frames whose count-vs-threshold curve has a plateau) | series coverage without human clicks | 2 GPU hours per round | uncertain; supplement to human clicks (S3 shows count-as-loss turns the detector into a density regressor) |
| series-balanced sampling, stronger scale and contrast augmentation, GroupNorm or frozen BN | part of the transfer gap | 1 GPU hour | small alone |

Gate before any of it enters a submission: CD MAPE on val 892 at or below the CNN's 6.32 (S2's gate).
Do not revisit capacity, resolution, augmentation, calibration, seeds or longer training for the
whole-image regressor; every one is measured at 0.03 or less.
